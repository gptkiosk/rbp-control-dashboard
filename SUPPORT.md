# Public support and maintenance

Use [Discussions](https://github.com/gptkiosk/rbp-control-dashboard/discussions) for community questions, ideas and fork announcements. Use [Issues](https://github.com/gptkiosk/rbp-control-dashboard/issues/new/choose) for reproducible bugs or concrete proposals; include sanitized environment details and the source revision. See [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [SECURITY](SECURITY.md).

This is an as-is, fork-friendly MIT project. Public issues, PRs and Discussions are open for interaction, but no ongoing upstream development, support, turnaround time or PR review is promised. Do not treat an automated acknowledgment, CI result or agent comment as a maintainer commitment. Maintain your own fork for dependable development and updates.
