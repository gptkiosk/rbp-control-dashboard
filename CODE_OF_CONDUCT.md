# Respectful participation

Be considerate, specific and constructive. Discuss ideas and behavior without harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing or personal attacks. Respect privacy and never post secrets or private logs. Keep technical reports reproducible and distinguish measured facts from assumptions.

Maintainers may edit, remove or close abusive/off-topic content and restrict participation. For sensitive conduct concerns use an already-established private maintainer contact; if none is available, ask only how to contact privately without posting personal details. Security vulnerabilities use SECURITY.md. This policy creates no guaranteed moderation or response service.
