# Security policy

Private vulnerability reporting is enabled for [RCD](https://github.com/gptkiosk/rbp-control-dashboard/security/advisories/new). Use the Security tab’s **Report a vulnerability** form and confirm it is private before entering details. Never post credentials, private logs, personal identifiers or sensitive vulnerability information in public issues, PRs or Discussions. If the private form is unavailable, ask only for a private contact route without disclosing details.

Describe the affected revision, component, sanitized reproduction and impact. Keep each repository’s reports separate: RCD reports do not automatically cover the other dashboard or a third-party runtime. Fork owners establish their own reporting channel and policy.

MIT licensing and public collaboration do not promise ongoing maintenance, support, a response deadline or security fixes. Review all deployments independently and maintain your own fork if needed. Experimental/unreleased features are not a supported production security boundary. Operator decisions, credential rotation and publication remain explicit actions.
